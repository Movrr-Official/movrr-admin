## Legal rules used for this decision

The [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens retention guidance](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/over-privacy/persoonsgegevens/bewaren-van-persoonsgegevens) confirms there is no universal GDPR retention interval. Purposes, necessary fields and a justified period must be recorded. The [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679) requires minimisation, storage limitation and a lawful basis; Article 17 exceptions include applicable legal duties and necessary legal claims. An exception is not itself a standalone Article 6 basis. Identifiable restricted evidence and keyed pseudonyms remain personal data. A truly anonymous result needs a supported re-identification assessment, not just removal of a name.

[Belastingdienst's current accounting guidance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/administratie/) gives seven years for basic accounting records; the clock starts when current business relevance ends. Continuing obligations can defer that start. Specific ten-year classes apply only if relevant. This does not establish which MOVRR point, redemption or equipment fields form the statutory administration. Do not retain rider profiles or GPS for seven years merely because a financial category exists.

For non-statutory retention relying on Article 6(1)(f), the controller must document interest, necessity and balancing, as described by the [AP lawful-basis guidance](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/themas/basis-avg/avg-algemeen/grondslagen-avg-uitgelegd). Article 6(1)(b) is available only for necessary performance of a contract with the rider, not merely MOVRR's advertiser contract. An ordinary civil limitation period is not a universal duty to retain every record for that duration. Refer unresolved claims/limitation and contested point-disposition questions for qualified review rather than invent a period.

Request handling must track the Article 12 response deadline: normally one month from receipt, with a justified extension of up to two further months and timely notice where applicable. This is a service deadline, not an evidence-retention period. Internal review deadlines do not replace it. The founder/co-founder monitored `privacy@movrr.nl` channel remains available; its existence does not establish an untested automatic notification path.
